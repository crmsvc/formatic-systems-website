Formatic Labs — Preview

Culture

Emergence from instinct.

Labs is where we follow curiosity instead of a roadmap. No revenue target, no client, nothing to justify — just ideas we want to mess with and whatever crawls out the other side. This is the first preview.

Culture is a digital petri dish. A few hundred agents, a handful of species, and one hidden rulebook deciding who's drawn to whom and who flees whom. No agent is told to build anything — yet from pure local instinct, approach and avoid, membranes form, colonies bloom, and organisms chase prey that flees them. You don't design the result. You set the conditions and cultivate what emerges.

Every seed is a different rulebook, so every seed grows a different lifeform. Roll a new one, nudge the conditions, and when something stops you — share it.

The Copy Share Link button rebuilds your exact organism — same seed, same settings, same colors — for anyone who opens the link.
