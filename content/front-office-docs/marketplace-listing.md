The Marketplace is the page where a business owner connects a tool with one click. Listings for other platforms are not open yet. You can send a request now; a person reads every one, and sending it does not list your platform.

## What the Marketplace is

The Marketplace is the page where a business owner sees what can be connected with one click.

## What is in it today

Connections we built ourselves:

- Google Calendar and Gmail
- QuickBooks Online
- GoHighLevel

Jobber and Housecall Pro are planned.

## Listings for other platforms

Listings for other platforms are a later step. The listing process is not open yet, and we are collecting requests now so we know who to talk to when it is.

Every request is read by a person. Sending one does not list your platform, and a listing is not needed for a business to connect you: any owner can already add your server by its address, as described in [Connect your platform](/docs/connect-your-platform).

## What to send

The request form asks for:

- Your company name and a contact email.
- The address of your MCP server (https).
- A link to your documentation.
- What your tools do: which only look things up, which change or send something, and what a service business would use them for.

Product names belong to their owners. Naming a product on these pages means a connection to it exists or is planned; it does not mean that company endorses or is affiliated with Formatic Systems.

## Ask to be listed

Fill in the request form on the Marketplace listing page. The request goes to Formatic for review, and sending it does not list your platform.
